About me

I build things people can actually use.

Most of my projects start as a simple idea — something I want for myself, or something that would help someone else — and end as a real product live on the internet that anyone can open and use.

I build by vibe coding: I direct AI tools like Muse, Claude, and ChatGPT to build apps, games, and websites, then I test, fix, and polish them until they work the way I pictured. I deploy everything with GitHub and Vercel, so every project has real code and a real live link — not just a mockup.

Design matters just as much as code to me. I love designing in Canva and I'm very creative — I care about clean layouts, strong colors, and making things look professional, not thrown together.
